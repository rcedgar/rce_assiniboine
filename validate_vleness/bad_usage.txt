usage: vleness [--threads N] [--rnafold | --tsv I,J | --lsc]
               [--keep-full-label] [--log FILE] [--maxerror N]
               input output.tsv

Score each circular dot-bracket. pct_bulge is the percent of the circle
in unpaired gaps inside composite stems (helices joined across at most
five unpaired nucleotides on a strand). S = (pct_bulge - 12) / 5.21.
pct_bulge is printed with one decimal place and S with four.

The first byte selects the format: '>' is RNAfold, anything else is TSV.
--rnafold forces three-line RNAfold records (label, sequence, structure).
A trailing Vienna energy on the structure line is ignored. The label is
cut at the first whitespace unless --keep-full-label is set.
--tsv I,J reads a TSV whose 1-based columns are the label and the
dot-bracket (default 1,2). A first row whose structure field is not a
dot-bracket is a header and is skipped.
--lsc reads label, MFE, dot-bracket, MFE, dot-bracket and writes
label, length, pct_bulge1, pct_bulge2, S1, S2, max(S1, S2) with no header.
LSC is also chosen automatically when the first TSV row has dot-brackets
in fields 3 and 5. --rnafold, --tsv, and --lsc are mutually exclusive.

Format problems are warnings on stderr. --log FILE also records them,
with the start time and the finish time. The run stops after more than
--maxerror warnings (default 1000) and exits non-zero. Rows accepted
before that stop are still written.
--threads defaults to the processor count in /proc/cpuinfo.
